Module 3 — I: Investigate the Facts

Gathering Evidence Objectively

Gather first. Conclude later.

Once the issue is framed, you gather the information needed to answer it. The goal is to collect the facts objectively — before forming a view. Different concerns call for different evidence, but most of it is already recorded somewhere in the plant.
