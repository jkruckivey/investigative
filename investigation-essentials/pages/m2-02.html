Module 2 — F: Frame the Issue

Activity: Frame the Question

Pick the best-framed investigation question

For each concern, choose the version that is objective, specific, and answerable with evidence. This activity is for practice and is not scored.

Concern 1

"That new operator is careless — they keep messing up the line."

Concern 2

"Someone skipped a food safety check and now we have a problem."

Concern 3

"He obviously didn't wash his hands — he never does."

ⓘ
Notice the pattern: the best questions name the specific conduct, the date or occasion, and the standard — and never assume the answer.
