Supervisor Job Aid

FIND Investigation Checklist

Keep this handy for any workplace investigation

F — Frame the Issue

  • ☐ What concern am I investigating?
  • ☐ What question needs to be answered?

I — Investigate the Facts

  • ☐ What evidence should I review?
  • ☐ Who do I need to speak with?

N — Narrow Down What Happened

  • ☐ What facts have been established?
  • ☐ What root causes may have contributed?

D — Document and Decide

  • ☐ Have I documented the facts?
  • ☐ Is my conclusion supported by evidence?
  • ☐ Have I identified the appropriate next steps?
Remember

FIND before you decide. The same skills apply to attendance, performance, conduct, GMP, food safety, and safety concerns alike — focus on facts, not assumptions.
