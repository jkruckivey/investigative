Module 4 — N: Narrow Down What Happened

Activity: Case Analysis

Sort the statements: fact, assumption, or root cause?

Scenario — Quality Defect

Multiple packages are rejected due to incorrect labels. A supervisor believes a specific employee loaded the wrong labels. The employee says the labels were already loaded when they arrived. Shift notes indicate a label change occurred earlier in the day.

Drag each statement into Established fact, Unsupported assumption, or Possible root cause. This activity is for practice and is not scored.
